Your AirPods case is a mouse.

Download for Mac

Free and open source. macOS 15 or later. Tested with AirPods 4.

Open it.

AirPods already carry motion sensors for spatial audio. Leave them in the case with the lid open and Automatic Ear Detection off, and they keep reporting.

Aim it.

Hold it like a laser pointer. Rest your elbow and move only your wrist, in short smooth moves.

Tap to click.

Stop, then tap the case. Taps only count on a still hand, so moving around won't click by accident.
